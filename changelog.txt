Version: 0.2.0
Date: 2026-10-08
  Features:
    - Added setting to disable the unnecessary second technologies (Oil Gathering, Uranium Mining) and merge them into the main ones.
    - Added a setting per Space Age planet (Vulcanus, Gleba, Fulgora, Aquilo) that pre-researches the planet discovery and the technologies needed to craft its ingredients on new saves, for starting on another planet. Without the merge setting it also researches the trigger technologies (except science packs), and Aquilo includes the other three planets. Only shown when Space Age is loaded.
    - With Space Age, the merge setting (renamed from the vanilla research replace setting) also moves each planet's trigger technologies (except the science pack ones) into its planet discovery technology. Technologies that required them now require the discovery.
  Changes:
    - Updated for Factorio 2.1.
    - Fixed spelling in the locale.
  Bugfixes:
    - The locale file is now included in the packaged zip.
    - Starting research no longer errors if a technology is missing.
